<p>
Mound is a design and fabrication house based in Richmond VA on Powhatan land, born in a global pandemic and designing with end-times flair. We recognize a need for a Post-Lifestyle design objective; to design outside of fickle trends which promote consumption above all. We listen to those early modernist shamans who said design can better someone's life and develop their inner self. Mound designs for the tomb-set society, those skeptical old souls who think the word "new" is a myth invented to sell cereal. <i>Mound produces grave goods you can live with.</i>
