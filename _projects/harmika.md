<h2>HARMIKA</h2>
<p>
Executed in walnut, maple, and brass, Harmika draws inspiration from the Buddhist practice of stupa-building. This information can be taken or it can be left. Her eight stepped ziggurat represents the Noble Eightfold Path, the four legs the Four Noble Truths, and her Double Vajra-like lamp the Tree of Life connecting heaven and earth. In her pendant form, Harmika also includes three rows of brass bells, symbolizing the Three Jewels. Although this item is not fully custom, different dimensions and materials can be requested.
